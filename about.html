About Vegas Vision

Vegas Vision is a general contracting and interior fit-out company based in Qatar.

We provide design, finishing, renovation and maintenance services for villas, restaurants, coffee shops, shops and offices in Doha and across the country.

Because we handle gypsum, painting, carpentry, flooring and ceilings ourselves, one company can take your project from start to handover.
